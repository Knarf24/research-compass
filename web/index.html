How the match % works

The score shows how well a program fits your profile. It is not your chance of being accepted, and no AI guesses it: it follows fixed rules you can check, and the reasons and warnings under each program show exactly what counted.

  • Field (up to 35): the program covers your major (35) or another field you picked (25).
  • Interests (up to 20): your research interests appear in the program's description.
  • Class year (15): your year is listed as eligible (8 if the program doesn't say).
  • Eligibility (15): confirmed open to you (7 if it needs checking, for example for international students).
  • Paid, deadline more than two weeks away, location (5 each). Programs for HBCU students get a bonus if you attend one.
  • Limits: if you don't meet a stated requirement (class year, citizenship or a GPA minimum) the score is capped at 40 and it's hidden by "Only ones I qualify for". If we can't confirm one (no GPA in your profile, international eligibility unclear) it's capped at 90. So 100 means everything we can check lines up.
